Bulk Import Students from Excel

Download the template, fill in your students (one row each), then upload it here. You'll get a chance to check and fix anything before it's actually saved. The "Class" column accepts anything — numbers like "6", or names like "Nursery", "B-I", "T-6 A" — whatever your school actually calls it.

⚠️ Excel tip: if your class names include hyphens or numbers (like "T-6" or "B-I"), select that column and set its format to Text before typing (Home → Number format → Text). Otherwise Excel can silently turn them into dates.
